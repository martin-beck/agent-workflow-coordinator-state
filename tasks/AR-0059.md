## Objective

Implement mutation one phase at a time, beginning with backup and durable
outcome recording, while preserving rejection-only behavior for every phase not
yet proven.

## Acceptance evidence

- Every effect captures and verifies session/authority identity before and
  after, holds the ordered barrier, and fsyncs a durable outcome.
- Git and SQLite paths are tested separately for failure before/during/after
  effect, close/fsync uncertainty, process death, reopen, and recovery.
- Each externally visible point has either a verified functional old runtime or
  verified functional new runtime; no partial authority state is observable.
- Selector publication, replacement, commit, apply, and rollback are promoted
  as separate reviewed gates, never in one broad change.
