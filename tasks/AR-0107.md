immutable issue number/URL, privacy-safe issue digest, members, dependency
edges and exactly one finalizer. A planner supplies a

required AR. Reject duplicate bindings, missing finalizers, cycles,
cross-project members, incomplete scope, and finalizers not ordered after all
members. Preserve CAS, leases, locks, bindings, views and Git/SQLite parity.
