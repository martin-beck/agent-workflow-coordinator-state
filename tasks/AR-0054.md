## Objective

Establish trace-preserving correspondence between the bounded formal model and
the merged executable evidence, including ordering, fencing, ambiguity,
functional availability, and recovery. Produce an explicit decision artifact
that keeps every mutation path disabled unless all required refinement and
hostile evidence gates pass.

## Acceptance evidence

- Exact model/config/source revisions are recorded and reproducible.
- Fast PR TLC and weekly full TLC scopes are distinct, with timeout/orphan
  handling and no false-success classification.
- Every claimed model transition maps to executable evidence; unsupported
  transitions remain explicit non-claims.
- The decision artifact is fail-closed and preserves coordinator usability on
  every failure path.
- Full local/hosted quality gates, post-merge Formal/Verify, and durable
  reconciliation pass.

## Non-goals

This AR does not enable selector publication, runtime replacement, commit,
apply, rollback, or release publication. Any enablement requires a subsequent
independent mutation AR after this gate is accepted.
