## Outcome

The new bounded path is demonstrated end to end, its evidence is accurately
rendered, and automated regression checks prevent a future change from silently
making routine formal validation unbounded or blocking.

## Acceptance

- Exercise the full path from exact candidate through receipt rendering and
  coordinator status projection.
- Verify duration/storage budget enforcement and typed remediation on a
  controlled failure.
- Verify stress jobs remain advisory and independently cancellable.
- Document migration of legacy evidence and compatibility behavior, with
  property or bounded-model tests for the critical lifecycle rules.
- Keep all acceptance evidence locally reproducible; external authentication,
  signatures, and independent review are optional, never progression gates.
