## Objective

Define immutable, typed recovery authorization evidence joining the verified
backup, restore round-trip, barrier, fencing, backend, runtime, and selector
facts. The evidence must remain diagnostic-only and cannot authorize rollback.

## Acceptance evidence

- Missing, stale, forged, contradictory, or uncertain recovery evidence rejects
  before any authority effect.
- Evidence is bound to one operation, backend, target, fencing token, barrier
  revision, and known-good runtime identity.
- No rollback, apply, commit, selector publication, or runtime replacement is
  performed by this AR.
- Every failure preserves a usable, write-closed coordinator and the last
  known-good authority.
- Hostile tests, formal mapping, full coverage, exact-head hosted gates,
  post-merge Formal/Verify, and durable reconciliation are complete.

## Non-goals

Rollback/apply dispatch, selector writes, runtime replacement, release
publication, and complete Python/TLA refinement remain disabled and require
later independent ARs.
