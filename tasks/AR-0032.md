## Objective

Define and implement the smallest additive contract that allows one logical upgrade session to span multiple durable journal revisions while preserving fail-closed behavior. This AR must not enable upgrade mutation or dispatch by itself.

## Required outcome

The coordinator can distinguish a legitimate multi-revision continuation from replay, stale state, journal replacement, fencing replacement, hash-chain tampering, or an ambiguous crash. All unsafe cases remain blocked and enter the existing permanent ambiguous-safe mode.

## Acceptance evidence

- Focused unit/property tests cover creation, continuation, exact replay, conflicting duplicate, sequence gap/regression, stale revision, authority/journal/barrier/fencing replacement, hash tampering, and crash-after-append-before-fsync.
- TLA/model checks cover append, replay, rejection, and crash actions with invariants for immutable identity, contiguous sequence, hash-chain integrity, terminal finality, and ambiguity containment.
- Legacy records remain readable for diagnosis but cannot authorize a mutation or dispatch transition.
- Fast merge formal gate, trusted post-merge gates, AWQ, scope, and exact-head evidence are green.
- Public evidence contains no private paths, credentials, or raw logs.

## Follow-up rule

Any newly discovered defect or missing contract is recorded as a child AR before implementation continues. Once this AR is complete, AR-0031 is resumed with the contract pinned and its mutation boundary remains disabled until its own evidence is complete.
