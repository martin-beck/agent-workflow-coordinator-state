The Coordinator currently integrates AWQ v0.32.0 while the latest published
AWQ release is v0.35.0. The upgrade must preserve the Coordinator's native
formal, release-sensitive, DCO, privacy, and exact-head gates even though the
new AWQ release removes its `formal-evidence` profile.

Acceptance requires:

- an owner-controlled `quality/workflow-trust.json` satisfying AWQ-GHA-002;
- regenerated `quality/awq.json` and `quality/awq.lock.json` satisfying
  AWQ-SUPPLY-001 and the v0.35.0 registry digest;
- immutable AWQ source pinning at the reviewed v0.35.0 commit in product
  metadata and CI;
- explicit Coordinator-owned formal gates retained in Verify and publication
  workflows;
- focused hostile tests for trust/profile drift and negative validation;
- independent exact-head review, hosted AWQ/scope/Verify gates, and a green
  post-merge Verify;
- public-safe durable evidence in this state repository.

Do not bundle this dependency/policy migration into a release tag, weaken
formal or release gates, or claim compatibility from a pin-only update.
