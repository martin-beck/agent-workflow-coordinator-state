Post-merge push verification must use the required bounded `pr-fast` tier.
Scheduled weekly and explicitly dispatched full-exhaustive runs remain advisory
sustained-quality signals with several-hour limits. Release-sensitive pull
requests and publication gates must retain blocking full evidence and exact-head
attestation requirements. A timeout, skip, or advisory failure must never be
represented as merge or release evidence.
