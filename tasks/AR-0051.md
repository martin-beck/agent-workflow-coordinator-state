## Objective

Define a typed, immutable commit authorization evidence contract that joins
backup, stage, selector, runtime, barrier, fencing, and durable revision facts
without authorizing or dispatching the commit operation.

## Acceptance evidence

- Missing, stale, forged, contradictory, or uncertain prerequisite evidence is
  rejected before any authority effect.
- The record is bound to one operation identity, backend identity, target,
  fencing token, barrier revision, selector digest, runtime manifest digest,
  and verified backup identity.
- No commit, selector publication, runtime replacement, apply, or rollback is
  performed by this AR.
- Failure leaves the prior known-good coordinator functional and write-closed.
- Hostile tests, formal mapping, full coverage, exact-head hosted gates,
  post-merge Formal/Verify, and durable reconciliation are complete.

## Non-goals

Commit/apply dispatch, selector writes, runtime replacement, rollback
authorization, release publication, and complete Python/TLA refinement remain
disabled and require later independent ARs.
