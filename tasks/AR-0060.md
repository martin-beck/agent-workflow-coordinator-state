## Objective

Perform the first supported upgrade from a previous release in a fresh state
checkout and prove every injected failure leaves a functional known-good or
validated new coordinator.

## Acceptance evidence

- Release-specific prerequisites, pre-upgrade, post-upgrade, and rollback
  steps are generated and validated from the proven contract.
- Fresh-clone identity, artifact ownership/digests, compatibility markers, and
  unsigned immutable-tag publication are verified.
- Every phase and failure cross-product is exercised, including rollback under
  the same held barrier and reopen/write resumption only after verification.
- Exact-head review, hosted quality/formal gates, post-merge verification,
  privacy-safe evidence, reconciliation, and live doctor all pass.
