## Outcome

Large state-space experiments remain available on a schedule or manual request
with strict containment and visible diagnostics, but their lifecycle cannot
block pull requests, candidate progression, or unrelated coordinator work.

## Acceptance

- Run only from an explicitly advisory workflow with dedicated capacity and
  independent cancellation/retry controls.
- Use the budgeted runner and preserve bounded diagnostic evidence on failure.
- Clearly label all outputs as exploratory, including partial or interrupted
  runs.
- Add tests or workflow checks proving advisory jobs cannot be selected by the
  normal minute-scale validation path.
