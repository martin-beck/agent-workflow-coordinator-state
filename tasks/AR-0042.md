## Objective

Connect the authenticated retained-runtime admission seam from AR-0041 to one real
coordinator runtime invocation path. The consumer must use the fixed launcher only,
pass the retained entrypoint descriptor to the child process, close all handles on
success and failure, and classify any dispatch uncertainty as rejection-only.

## Acceptance evidence

- The consumer obtains `DispatchAdmission` from the selector-aware resolver.
- It calls `prepare_runtime_dispatch` immediately before launch and uses its exact
  `argv` and `pass_fds`; no caller-selected executable or script path is accepted.
- Selector, manifest, directory, entrypoint, and descriptor identity replacement
  between admission and launch fails closed.
- Child exit, timeout, spawn failure, descriptor-close failure, and malformed
  arguments leave no mutation and no leaked admission/entrypoint descriptors.
- Independent subprocess tests prove the selected release, not the adjacent checkout,
  is executed.
- Upgrade `apply` and `rollback` remain rejection-only until AR-0012 and AR-0031
  separately satisfy their barriers, rollback, and formal gates.

## Gates

Focused tests, full quality/coverage, formal evidence update, hosted checks, exact-head
review, post-merge Verify, and state-repository reconciliation are required.
