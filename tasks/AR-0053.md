## Objective

commit-prerequisite, and recovery-evidence seams into one executable,
fail-closed transition rehearsal. The rehearsal must prove ordering and
functional availability across success, process death, timeout, ambiguity, and
uncertain-read paths without performing authority mutation.

## Acceptance evidence

- Every phase consumes only the identity-bound evidence from its predecessor.
- Any failure preserves a known-good functional coordinator and leaves
  authority state write-closed.
- Ambiguous outcomes require explicit reconciliation and a newer fencing token.
- Formal correspondence covers the integrated ordering and failure matrix.
- Full suite, >=95% branch coverage, Ruff, mypy, hosted gates, post-merge
  Formal/Verify, and durable reconciliation pass.

rollback mutation, release publication, and a claim of complete Python/TLA
refinement remain disabled until a separately approved mutation AR.
