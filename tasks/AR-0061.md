## Objective

Implement and verify only selector publication and runtime replacement. Every
failure must leave the old runtime functional or enter explicit safe mode.

## Acceptance evidence

- Exact descriptor, selector, manifest, session, authority, and fencing identity
  is checked before and after publication.
- Atomic replace, directory fsync, close uncertainty, process death, reopen,
  and stale replacement races are independently tested.
- Durable outcome records are idempotent and unambiguous.
- Commit, apply, rollback, and release publication remain rejection-only.
