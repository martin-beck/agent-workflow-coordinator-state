SQLite mutation fencing required for safe upgrade admission. All uncertainty
must enter durable ambiguous safe mode; normal coordination remains usable.

  authority identity; caller callbacks and path-only identities are rejected.
- Strict barrier CAS transitions and repository-common -> control-store ->
  authority lock order are enforced before and after every effect.

  fenced, including database/WAL/SHM replacement and stale-owner races.

  tests prove writes resume only after verified `released`.
- Formal correspondence, independent review, hosted gates, post-merge checks,
  and live doctor pass; mutation dispatch remains disabled until separately
  authorized.
