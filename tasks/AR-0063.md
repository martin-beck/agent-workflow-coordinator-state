## Objective

Prove complete old/new runtime continuity and rollback behavior before any
release transition or upgrade mutation is authorized.

## Acceptance evidence

- Rollback requires verified durable backup, exact identity, and held barrier.
- Every injected failure ends in known-good old runtime, validated new runtime,
  or explicit safe mode; no partial authority state is observable.
- Fresh-clone prerequisites, generated pre/post steps, unsigned tag identity,
  reconciliation, hosted gates, and live doctor all pass.
