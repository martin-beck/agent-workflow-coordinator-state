Objective: determine why a small number of independent workers stall while
using the coordinator, quantify the contention across the real handoffctl
routes, and implement or recommend a materially more scalable design without
weakening authority, fencing, durability, or fail-closed behavior.

The investigation must use isolated workers and the necessary handoffctl route
for every coordinator access. Direct database/file mutation and synthetic lock
benchmarks that bypass handoffctl are not completion evidence.

Acceptance evidence: a reproducible concurrency workload; phase-level lock wait,
hold, timeout, queue-depth, and throughput measurements; identified long
critical sections and contention causes; a reviewed scalability design or
implementation with unchanged safety invariants; stress/failure tests at the
observed worker counts; independent exact-head review; hosted checks; and
reconciled coordinator state.
