This P0 successor AR owns GitHub issue #28 and is a prerequisite for enabling
any mutating upgrade or rollback command. It must preserve a functional
known-good or newly validated runtime at every externally observable boundary;
partial staging, ambiguous selector publication, validation failure, process
death, and rollback failure are first-class rejection/recovery cases.

Provenance: https://github.com/martin-beck/agent-workflow-coordinator/issues/28
("Upgrade: selector-aware launcher and authenticated versioned runtime", open
when this AR was created). The issue is the authoritative contract source;
this record narrows ownership and acceptance without weakening it.

The AR remains planned until the executable upgrade-engine and formal-model
contracts in AR-0007 and AR-0008 are accepted. Do not fabricate a launcher,
trust decision, selector transition, or runtime adapter to make a checkpoint
appear complete. Keep `handoffctl upgrade apply` and `handoffctl upgrade
rollback` fail-closed until all acceptance evidence is present.
