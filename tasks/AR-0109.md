Wire series membership into Coordinator lifecycle transitions using local
accepted evidence as the sole completion authority. Each transition may enqueue
one idempotent public-safe issue projection containing AR identity, evidence
reference or digest, and remaining work. Reopen, supersession, failure, and
rollback may enqueue corrected projections. Only the declared finalizer may
request an optional issue closure after all required members are locally done
with accepted evidence and dependencies are satisfied. A live identity check
applies only to that external close attempt. Authentication, hosted
availability, CI evidence, and external effects are supplementary; unavailable
or ambiguous effects become typed reconciliation states and never block local
lifecycle progress, acceptance, release, or finalization.
