Wire series membership into Coordinator lifecycle transitions. Each member
released `done` emits one idempotent issue update with AR identity, accepted
evidence reference/digest, exact merged/release identity when applicable, and
remaining work. Reopen, supersession, failure and rollback publish corrected
status. Only the declared finalizer can close, only after all required members
are done with accepted evidence, dependencies are satisfied, exact-head
qualification is current and live issue identity matches. Its final comment
lists ARs, implementation/release and CI evidence, exclusions and follow-ups.
