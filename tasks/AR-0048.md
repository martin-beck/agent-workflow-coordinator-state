## Objective

Make the `validate` phase consume a retained, authenticated runtime admission
and revalidate selector and manifest identity immediately before readiness is
reported. This must be a read-only capability: it cannot publish a selector,
replace a runtime, mutate authority state, or authorize commit/apply/rollback.

## Acceptance evidence

- The capability is constructed only from a retained `DispatchAdmission` and an
  immutable operation/session identity; caller-supplied paths or callbacks do
  not establish authority.
- Selector replacement, manifest replacement, digest drift, release mismatch,
  closed/forged admission, and uncertain reads reject before readiness evidence
  is returned.
- `UpgradeEngine` invokes the capability exactly once for `validate`, records
  only verified non-mutating evidence, and leaves unsupported mutation phases
  fail-closed.
- Hostile replacement and process-death/uncertain-read tests, formal mapping,
  full coverage, exact-head hosted gates, post-merge Formal/Verify, and durable
  reconciliation are complete before closure.

## Non-goals

Selector publication, runtime replacement, commit, apply, rollback, release
publication, and complete Python/TLA refinement remain disabled and unclaimed.
