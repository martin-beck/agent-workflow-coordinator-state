## Objective

Define a read-only runtime replacement admission contract that authenticates the
staged runtime tree, manifest, selector identity, backend identity, fencing
token, and durable revision immediately before any future replacement.

## Acceptance evidence

- Missing, stale, replaced, symlinked, digest-mismatched, foreign, or
  uncertain runtime evidence rejects before any authority effect.
- Admission is immutable and bound to one operation, selector, manifest,
  backend, fencing token, and barrier revision.
- No runtime replacement, selector publication, commit, apply, or rollback is
  performed by this AR.
- Every failure preserves the prior known-good runtime and leaves the
  coordinator usable.
- Hostile tests, formal mapping, full coverage, exact-head hosted gates,
  post-merge Formal/Verify, and durable reconciliation are complete.

## Non-goals

Runtime replacement, selector writes, commit/apply dispatch, rollback
authorization, release publication, and complete Python/TLA refinement remain
disabled and require later independent ARs.
