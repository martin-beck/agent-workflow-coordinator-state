## Outcome

Liveness is checked exhaustively over a deliberately bounded abstraction whose
removed dimensions are documented and covered by dedicated safety, recovery,
or parameterized checks.  The release tier completes within its declared
minute-scale budget on the supported development runner.

## Acceptance

- Add an explicit reduced model/configuration for liveness, separate from
  exploratory multi-process stress configurations.
- Document a dimension-by-dimension mapping from the production model to the
  reduced abstraction and the test/model that covers each omitted dimension.
- Preserve all applicable safety invariants and liveness properties in the
  reduced tier.
- Add bounded-model and regression tests that detect accidental expansion,
  property loss, or an invalid coverage mapping.
