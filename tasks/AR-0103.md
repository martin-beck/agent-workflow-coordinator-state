## Outcome

A formal execution is bound to an exact candidate revision and can be resumed,
cancelled, or superseded safely without holding a coordinator lease or blocking
unrelated work.  Only a receipt matching the current candidate can be used as
evidence.

## Acceptance

- Define start, running, completed, failed, cancelled, and superseded job
  states with revision fencing and atomic receipts.
- Ensure lease expiry, worker restart, and candidate replacement do not leave
  a permanent coordinator lock.
- Reject stale, incomplete, and wrong-candidate receipts deterministically.
- Test concurrent lifecycle operations and recovery paths with the formal-job
  resource classifications from AR-0102.
- Keep all acceptance evidence locally reproducible; external authentication,
  signatures, and independent review are optional, never progression gates.
