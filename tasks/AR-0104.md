## Outcome

Pull-request and candidate validation use the bounded formal tier, publish
exact-head receipts, and fail clearly when its declared contract is not met.
The normal workflow is predictable in minutes rather than hours.

## Acceptance

- Select the new tier explicitly in CI and prohibit accidental fallback to a
  large exploratory configuration.
- Verify revision, model/configuration digest, runner receipt, and budget
  result before reporting a passing formal check.
- Add deterministic CI fixtures for stale receipt, resource exhaustion, and
  timeout classifications.
- Record observed durations and enforce a defensible bounded timeout with
  concise remediation guidance.
