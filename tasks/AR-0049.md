## Objective

Define the smallest authority-neutral admission contract required before a
selector could ever be published. The contract must authenticate the retained
runtime, selector, manifest, fencing token, and durable revision, while keeping
the actual selector write disabled until its independent barrier and formal
contracts are complete.

## Acceptance evidence

- Forged, stale, replaced, ambiguous, or uncertain selector/runtime evidence is
  rejected before any authority effect.
- Admission is bound to one immutable operation identity, backend identity,
  fencing token, selector digest, manifest digest, and expected revision.
- No selector publication, runtime replacement, commit, apply, or rollback is
  performed by this AR.
- Every failure leaves the coordinator usable and the prior known-good runtime
  authoritative.
- Hostile tests, formal correspondence, full coverage, exact-head hosted gates,
  post-merge Formal/Verify, and durable reconciliation are complete.

## Non-goals

Selector writes, runtime replacement, commit/apply dispatch, rollback
authorization, release publication, and complete Python/TLA refinement remain
disabled and require subsequent independent ARs.
