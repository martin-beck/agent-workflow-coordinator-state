## Objective

target identity, and durable outcome capability. This AR must not connect the
capability to public handoffctl or enable general rollback dispatch.

## Acceptance evidence

  validated before any effect.
- Held/released/stale/ambiguous state, identity drift, process death, and
  uncertain outcome are hostile-tested with safe-mode classification.
- Independent review, exact-head hosted checks, post-merge Verify, and durable
  reconciliation pass at one product revision.
- The AI-generated model remains best-effort guidance; no source-to-model
  refinement proof is required.
- Public apply, rollback, release, and Dispatch remain rejection-only.
