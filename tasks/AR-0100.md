## Outcome

The coordinator distinguishes bounded development and release evidence from
open-ended exploration.  Labels, receipts, and documentation make the model
scope, time budget, and evidence strength explicit so a capped run is never
misrepresented as exhaustive over a larger model.

## Acceptance

- Define `pr-fast` (target under five minutes), `release-exhaustive` (target
  under fifteen minutes over a documented reduced model), and advisory
  `stress-exploration` tiers.
- Specify a machine-readable tier contract covering model/configuration,
  wall-time and storage budgets, failure classification, and claim language.
- Until AR-0102 is complete, make scheduler policy reject newly requested
  hour-scale TLC profiles; existing evidence remains historical or advisory.
- Migrate existing tier names and documentation compatibly, with tests for
  invalid or ambiguous tier declarations.
- Keep stress evidence informational: it must not retain coordinator authority
  or prevent ordinary development/release progression.
- Require no external signature, authentication service, GitHub approval, or
  independent reviewer for any tier to run, pass, merge, or release. Such
  evidence is optional and may only supplement locally verifiable results.
