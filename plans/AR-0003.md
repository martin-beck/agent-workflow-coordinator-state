# AR-0003: Release-upgrade contract and generator

Define the versioned data-only contract generated and published for every
coordinator release: supported source versions, target tag/commit/object,
vendor hashes, backend/schema compatibility, prerequisites, ordered bounded
steps, backup requirements, postconditions, rollback recipe, and evidence.

The interpreter must reject unknown fields, unsupported contract versions,
floating refs, arbitrary scripts, missing rollback data, and incomplete step
graphs. Generation must be deterministic and tested against every supported
release transition.
