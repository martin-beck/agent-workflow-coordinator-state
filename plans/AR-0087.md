# AR-0087: Barrier-before-quiescence admission

Implement the smallest missing lifecycle seam identified in the current upgrade
protocol: acquire and durably verify the maintenance barrier before admitting a
quiesced operation. Keep this as a bounded admission/evidence change; it must
not enable public apply, rollback, release, or Dispatch.

Acceptance requires exact identity binding for project, operation, barrier,
owner, revision, and fencing token; rejection of absent, stale, competing, or
uncertain barrier state; hostile ordering tests; independent review; and exact
head hosted checks. The generated formal model is best-effort AI guidance and
does not require a source-to-model refinement proof.
