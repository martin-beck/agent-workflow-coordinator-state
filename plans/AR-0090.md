# AR-0090: Verified launcher consumption

Connect the existing authenticated fixed-launcher seam to one real runtime
invocation path. The consumer must resolve the selected runtime, revalidate its
selector/manifest/directory/entrypoint identities immediately before execution,
pass caller arguments only, and classify child-process or identity uncertainty
as rejection. Keep public apply, rollback, release, and Dispatch rejection-only.

Acceptance requires hostile subprocess tests for normal selected-runtime
consumption, replacement, symlink/hard-link/path escape, selector drift, and
child failure; independent review; and exact-head hosted checks. The generated
formal model remains best-effort AI guidance; no source-to-model refinement
proof or authorization prerequisite is required.
