# AR-0103: Enforce live upgrade binding at production admission

## Objective

Make the live binding an actual production admission prerequisite. The command/phase boundary must obtain and validate the durable session, lock-domain scope, lease/recheck, and concrete backend identity before constructing any effect capability.

## Scope

- wire `LiveUpgradeBinding` into the production admission path;
- cross-check persisted binding identity on every load;
- exercise real durable SQLite and Git binding paths, not fabricated private objects;
- preserve fail-closed rejection for stale, foreign, released, ambiguous, missing, and process-death state;
- leave phase execution and commit/rollback orchestration to AR-0102.

## Completion

Complete only after exact-head independent review, hosted checks, fresh downstream consumption, and reconciled state evidence. A helper that is not consumed by production admission is insufficient.
