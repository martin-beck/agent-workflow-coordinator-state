# AR-0061 plan: selector and runtime publication gate

1. Define the exact forward selector/runtime mutation contract and durable
   outcome schema.
2. Bind publication to the verified descriptor, barrier identity, and current
   authority/session revision before and after the effect.
3. Inject replacement, fsync, close, process-death, reopen, and ambiguity
   failures in independent processes.
4. Prove known-good runtime continuity or explicit safe mode at every point;
   keep commit/apply/rollback disabled.
