# AR-0103: Issue-to-AR series end-to-end qualification and release

Release the workflow as a complete Coordinator capability. Prove a mock issue
journey creates a reviewed series, publishes its map, posts exactly one update
for each accepted AR completion, rejects premature closure, and allows one
complete finalizer closure. Deliver the command/operator documentation,
migration and permission guidance. Require end-to-end, property/fault,
Git/SQLite parity, privacy/generated-view and bounded formal evidence, then
independent review, exact-head CI, signed DCO release and immutable upstream
publication.
