# AR-0088: Forward barrier acquire/recheck/reopen lifecycle

Implement the smallest missing forward-barrier lifecycle seam after AR-0087.
Bind one durable barrier session through acquire, ordered rechecks, terminal
validation, and reopen. Keep all public apply, rollback, release, and Dispatch
paths rejection-only until the complete lifecycle is independently evidenced.

Acceptance requires immutable project/operation/barrier/owner/revision/fencing
identity, durable `held -> releasing -> released` transitions, hostile
process-death and uncertainty tests, independent review, and exact-head hosted
checks. The generated formal model is best-effort AI guidance; no
source-to-model refinement proof or authorization prerequisite is required.
