session and concrete Git or SQLite authority adapter. Produce an immutable
binding object that a later phase-orchestration continuation can pass to
UpgradeEngine; do not accept caller callbacks, synthetic evidence, or path-only
identities.

authority replacement, journal ambiguity, and uncertain effect outcomes into
durable safe mode. Require independent review, hosted checks, post-merge
verification, and reconciliation. AR-0102 owns production phase orchestration.

refinement proof or authorization prerequisite is required.
